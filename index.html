Changing tracker reloads the page. True scale (8th Wall) measures real distances but needs a few seconds of side-to-side motion first; without it, size follows the phone-height setting. Sharp camera and Map points apply to visloc-rs.

If objects slide against the background when you pan, adjust this until they stick. With visual tracking on, this also recalibrates the tracker (it restarts) and is saved for this phone.

Set this to how high you hold the phone. With 8th Wall it sets the scale of the world when tracking starts (reload to apply). With visloc-rs it places the floor until one is detected.
