Calibration

Visual 6DoF tracks the camera image so objects stay pinned as you walk. Sharp camera looks better but may track worse. The first two reload the page.

If objects slide against the background when you pan, adjust this until they stick. With visual tracking on, this also recalibrates the tracker (it restarts) and is saved for this phone.

Set this to how high you hold the phone. Until the floor is detected, the floor is assumed this far below the phone, so lower it if objects float and raise it if they sink.

Steps + GPS moves you one stride per detected step and lets GPS correct slowly. Anchors hold still whenever you do.
